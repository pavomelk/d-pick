Incremental Segmented Date Parser

Served by Vite and loaded as plain ES modules — nothing is transpiled or minified.

Type a date in any order the parser can read (25524, 25/5/2024, May 25, 2024). ↓/↑ walk the readings, Enter confirms, and leaving the field resets a value that is not a full date.

The cut is committed as you type, so a separator or a leading zero is how you steer it: 1225 is day 12, month 2, year 2005, while 1 2 25 is day 1, month 2, year 2025 and 02305 is day 02, month 3, year 05. Separators (. / - , and spaces) are interchangeable.
