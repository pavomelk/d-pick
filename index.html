Incremental Segmented Date Parser

Served by Vite and loaded as plain ES modules — nothing is transpiled or minified.

Type a date in any order the parser can read (25524, 25/5/2024, May 25, 2024). ↓/↑ walk the readings, Enter or leaving the field confirms a reading the parser can resolve, and Esc cancels the edit. Leaving with an unfinished value resets it, and Tab moves focus without entering the field. A month prefix is shown as soon as it is determined: N gives November, while J starts at June and ↓ walks July and January.

The cut is committed as you type, so a separator or a leading zero is how you steer it: 1225 is day 12, month 2, year 2005, while 1 2 25 is day 1, month 2, year 2025 and 02305 is day 02, month 3, year 05. A leading digit 4–9 selects month-first: 5 is May and 4/5/2024 is April 5, 2024; a leading 0–3 or a month name keeps day-first. Separators (. / - , and spaces) are interchangeable; Tab is not one of them.
